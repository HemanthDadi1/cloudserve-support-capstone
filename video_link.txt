Title: CloudServe Support: answering only what the documentation can prove

One line: A support system that sends a reply only when the reply can be checked against CloudServe's documentation, and passes every other ticket to a person with context and a logged reason.

Description: CloudServe asked for a chatbot. Discovery on 500 past tickets showed that most answers already existed but weren't reaching customers or agents. This video walks through that finding, the design it led to, a live run on real tickets showing a success, an escalation and a guardrail block, and the unattended validation run. It also covers results against target, including the shortfalls, and governance.
